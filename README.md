# Pocket-Triage-demo

Pocket Triage is a volunteer-led initiative focused on providing offline-first physical and mental first aid guidance for people in situations where medical support may be unavailable or delayed. The goal is to offer practical, easy-to-follow guidance that can help stabilize a situation until professional help becomes available.
